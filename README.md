# 🎣 Deepwell

**Deepwell** is a minimalistic fishing simulator built around depth, rarity, and quiet progression.

Cast your line into unknown waters and see what rises from below.  
The deeper you go, the rarer the catch.

---

## 🌊 About The Game

Deepwell is designed to be simple on the surface — but layered underneath.

There are no overwhelming mechanics, no cluttered UI, no unnecessary noise.  
Just you, the water, and what waits beneath it.

Progression is steady.  
Rarity scales with depth.  
Luck influences what you pull from the deep.

The well is endless.

---

## 🎲 Core Features

- 🎣 Simple and clean fishing mechanics  
- 🌊 Depth-based rarity system  
- 💎 Increasingly rare catches the deeper you go  
- ⚙️ Lightweight and optimized design  
- 📈 Progression-focused gameplay  

---

## 🐟 Rarity System

Fish are distributed across depth tiers.

The deeper you cast:
- Common catches become less frequent
- Rare catches become possible
- Legendary depths unlock hidden finds

Luck may influence outcomes — but the deep always decides.

---

## 🧩 Design Philosophy

Deepwell was created with three goals in mind:

1. Simplicity
2. Atmosphere
3. Rewarding rarity

No unnecessary bloat.
No forced complexity.
Just depth.
