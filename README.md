# Deepwell

### Cast deep. Reel carefully. Discover what waits below.

**Deepwell** is a pixel-art fishing game built for the web.

Cast your line, master the reeling minigame, discover **120 real fish species**, and fill out your entire **Deepwell Journal**.

</div>

---

## Fishing

Cast into the water and wait for a bite.

Once something takes the hook, keep control of the line and successfully reel it in through Deepwell's fishing minigame.

Different catches offer their own challenge, so landing a fish isn't always guaranteed.

## 120 Real Fish Species

Deepwell features **120 real-world fish species** to discover.

Keep fishing, encounter new species, and see how much of the collection you can uncover.

## Deepwell Journal

Every species you discover is recorded in the **Deepwell Journal**.

Use it to track:

* Fish you've discovered
* Fish you're still missing
* Your progress toward completing the full collection
